\section{Evaluation}\label{sec:txid-evaluation}

We evaluate the concrete instantiation of Section~\ref{sec:txid-instantiation},
benchmarking each relation's proof and verification cost individually before
reporting end-to-end figures for the full protocol.

\subsection{Experimental evaluation}\label{sec:txid-eval-experimental}

All experiments were conducted on an Apple M1 Pro with 16GB RAM using Go's
benchmarking framework. The algebraic construction is implemented as a research
prototype without batching, precomputation, or multi-exponentiation
optimizations; benchmarks represent conservative performance estimates.

For the proof of knowledge of a signature from the BBS+ signature scheme
($\rel_4$ and $\rel_9$), the prover costs are 3 scalar multiplications, 2
multi-exponentiations in $\group{1}$, and 5 scalar responses. The verifier
costs are 2 pairing evaluations and 3 multi-exponentiations in $\group{1}$.
For the epoch key proof, $\rel_5$, the prover costs are 2 exponentiations in
$\group{1}$ and 1 scalar response. The verifier cost is 4 exponentiations in
$\group{1}$.

For the transaction tag proof, $\rel_8$, the prover and verifier costs for the
commitment and epoch key correctness and tag PRF correctness are 2
exponentiations in $\group{1}$, $\ell+1$ exponentiations in $\group{2}$, and 1
pairing evaluation. For the range check, the prover costs are $O(\ell)$
exponentiations plus the inner product argument of $O(\ell)$ group operations,
and the verifier cost is $O(\ell)$ exponentiations.

We note that the proof of $\rel_4$ is computed once at registration and once
per Grant invocation, and the proof of $\rel_5$ once per epoch.  The proofs of
$\rel_9$ and $\rel_8$ are computed per transaction.

  Proof generation completes in a few milliseconds and verification in under
  10ms even without optimisation, demonstrating practical deployability.  For
  context, Idemix~\cite{CCS:CamVan02}, a widely deployed anonymous credential
  system over BLS12-381, requires approximately 4ms for signing and 3ms for
  verification without tracing support. Our construction achieves comparable
  verification cost while additionally supporting epoch-bound tracing. A
  hash-based instantiation using zk-SNARKs, achieving sub-millisecond
  verification at the cost of a trusted setup, is benchmarked in the full
  version of this paper.
