\section{Summary}\label{sec:txid-summary}

\begin{itemize}

  \item This chapter addresses the gap between fully transparent ledgers and
    long-term view keys: existing options offer either no privacy, or
    unconditional and permanent auditor visibility.

  \item We introduce tracing: users grant an auditor the ability to trace their
    transactions during a single chosen epoch, with authorisation expiring
    automatically once that epoch ends.

  \item We formalise tracing as an ideal functionality capturing anonymity,
    non-repudiation, and permission expiration, and give a black-box
    construction from signatures, commitments, pseudorandom functions, and NIZK
    proofs.

  \item We instantiate the construction in pairing-based groups with a
    transparent setup, using the BBS+ signature scheme so the registration
    authority never learns a user's tracing key.

  \item The concrete instantiation is practical: proof generation completes in
    a few milliseconds, and verification in under 10ms, even without
    optimisation.

\end{itemize}
