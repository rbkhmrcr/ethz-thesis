\section{Evaluation}\label{sec:offline-evaluation}
\suppressfloats[t]

\begin{outline}
  \item only users' wallets are resource constrained, so integrate $\OP$ into a mobile wallet and measure on two phones with a smart card as secure element
  \item every algorithm also measured separately in a server-side implementation, for the intermediary, central bank, registration authority and auditor
\end{outline}

\begin{outline}
  \item phones: Samsung Galaxy A34 (Android 14), iPhone 13 mini (iOS 17.6.1), cryptographic library in Go
  \item secure element: Javacard applet on an NXP smart card, Smart MX D600 chip, JCOP 4.5, NFC to the phone \cite{javacard}; why a smart card rather than emulation, TEE or platform HSM (old text had this)
  \item applet signs only for the commitment it generated on receiving a token, and once spent only for the single destination it first signed; this enforces single use
  \item wallet to payee over Bluetooth Low Energy, to intermediary over HTTPS; point to \Cref{fig:offline-withdraw-seq,fig:offline-pay-seq}
  \item server side: Go, gnark-crypto 0.21.0, BLS12-381 \cite{gnark-crypto}; MacBook Air, Apple M2, 16\,GB RAM
  \item medians of $100$ runs, $10$ warm-up calls on the server; IQR within $3\%$ of median for most operations, at most $13\%$ on phones and card, $10\%$ on server
  \item secure element replaced by an untimed stub on the server
  \item measure with and without the audit ciphertext, to separate cost of auditing from cost of payment
\end{outline}

\begin{outline}
  \item point to \Cref{tab:offline-timings,fig:offline-phone-steps} for wallet steps on phones and card
  \item withdrawal and payer side each under half a second on both phones
  \item most of it is the card: about $230\,\mathrm{ms}$ to generate a pseudonym, $370\,\mathrm{ms}$ for its share of the transfer signature, NFC included
  \item auditing adds $9\,\mathrm{ms}$ (Android) and $2\,\mathrm{ms}$ (iOS) to the payer's phone, under $3\%$ of payer total
\end{outline}

\begin{outline}
  \item point to \Cref{fig:offline-hops-times,fig:offline-hops-sizes}: growth with chain length
  \item verification linear: each hop adds one signature of knowledge and one audit proof
  \item $32$ hops verify in under a second on both phones: $883\,\mathrm{ms}$ Android, $577\,\mathrm{ms}$ iOS, with auditing
  \item from four hops on, auditing costs the payee a further $10$ to $20\%$
  \item payments grow $912$ bytes per hop with auditing, $688$ without
\end{outline}
