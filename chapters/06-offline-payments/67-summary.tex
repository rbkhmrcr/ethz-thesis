\section{Summary}\label{sec:offline-summary}

In this chapter, we gave a transferable offline payment scheme, whose payments are
anonymous and whose withdrawals cannot be linked to deposits. We proved that
$\OP$ securely realises $\idfu{\algo{op}}$ when its building blocks have the
properties of \Cref{thm:offline-construction-security}, and that its
instantiation does so under the DL, DDH, $q$-SDH and $q$-MSDH assumptions in the
random oracle model. A smart card authorises a payment in under
$400\,\mathrm{ms}$, the payer's phone needs under $50\,\mathrm{ms}$, and a payee
verifies a chain of $32$ hops in under a second, with payments growing by $912$
bytes per hop.

Divisibility remains future work, and revocation offline is limited to expiry by epochs.
Replacing the auditor with a different scheme in an efficient way is also future work.
