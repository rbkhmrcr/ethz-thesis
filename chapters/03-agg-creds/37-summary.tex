\section{Summary}\label{sec:agg-creds-summary}

\begin{outline}
  \item the gap: later systems decentralise issuance, but the issuers as a whole stay responsible for all attributes; we target distinct issuers with no coordination between issuers, or between RA, issuers and verifiers
  \item what we built: aggregate anonymous credentials, credentials from independent issuers combined by the user into one compact credential, one proof, stateless issuers
  \item the key point: every scheme already assumes users' keys are certified; naming that party and giving it one job (binding a key to a tag) adds no trust, it learns a key and a tag and is never contacted again, and it is what lets issuers stay uncoordinated and stateless
  \item first instance of the wider argument of the thesis (link to the intro)
  \item ART-Sign makes it concrete: both bind a user to one tag at registration; where the tag is the key, issuers remember what they signed; where the RA signs tag and key together, they do not
\end{outline}

\begin{outline}
  \item optional, broader: many systems contain an unnamed role (whoever certifies a key, assigns an identifier, admits a participant); naming it with a narrow capability can remove coordination
  \item example settings: wallets with credentials from unrelated national registries, or from employers, universities, health services
\end{outline}

\begin{outline}
  \item instantiation from the AGHO SPS scheme and our BLS-based $\ATS$, with a special-purpose Sigma proof; headline numbers from \Cref{sec:agg-creds-evaluation} (at $n = 128$, $k = 64$: present 20.1ms, verify 31.1ms; costs scale with $k$, not $n$)
\end{outline}

\begin{outline}
  \item open directions: GGM is needed only through AGHO; a rerandomisable SPS under a standard assumption would remove it, at the cost of larger registration signatures
  \item concrete security analysis: parameter sizes for a given number of issuers and presentations
  \item revocation: epoch-based is sketched; revocation of a whole user through their tag and of single attributes needs policies as well as mechanisms
  \item issuer hiding: issuer keys are used in the clear, which can reveal information about the user; hiding which issuer from an accepted set would remove the last leak
\end{outline}
