\section{Evaluation}\label{sec:txid-evaluation}

We evaluate the concrete instantiation of Section~\ref{sec:txid-instantiation},
benchmarking each relation's proof and verification cost individually before
reporting end-to-end figures for the full protocol.

All experiments were conducted on an Apple M2 with 16GB RAM using Go's
benchmarking framework. The algebraic construction is implemented as a research
prototype without batching, precomputation, or multi-exponentiation
optimisations. The benchmarks below represent conservative performance
estimates.

For the proof of knowledge of a signature from the BBS+ signature scheme
($\rel_4$ and $\rel_9$), which now range over the three secrets
$(\sk,\utk,\utk')$ certified by the credential, the prover costs are 4 scalar
multiplications, 2 multi-exponentiations in $\group{1}$ (of sizes three and
two), and 8 scalar responses. The verifier costs are 2 pairing evaluations and
4 multi-exponentiations in $\group{1}$. For the epoch key proof, $\rel_5$, the
prover costs are 2 exponentiations in $\group{1}$ and 1 scalar response. The
verifier cost is 5 exponentiations in $\group{1}$, the extra exponentiation
over the credential proof coming from checking the disclosed $\utk'$ against
the credential directly.

For the transaction tag proof, $\rel_8$, following the Dodis-Yampolskiy
instantiation of $\FT$, the prover cost for the commitment, epoch-key term, and
tag correctness is one 3-base multi-exponentiation and two single
exponentiations in $\group{1}$, up to three exponentiations in $\group{T}$, one
exponentiation in $\group{2}$ to derive the tag, and one pairing evaluation.
The verifier cost is one pairing evaluation, one 3-base multi-exponentiation
and five single exponentiations in $\group{1}$, and four exponentiations in
$\group{T}$. For the range check, the prover costs are $O(\ell)$
exponentiations plus the Bulletproofs inner product argument of $O(\ell)$ group
operations, and the verifier cost is $O(\ell)$
exponentiations~\cite{SP:BBBPWM18}.

We note that the proof of $\rel_4$ is computed once at registration and once
per Grant invocation, and the proof of $\rel_5$ once per epoch.  The proofs of
$\rel_9$ and $\rel_8$ are computed per transaction.

    Proof generation completes in a few milliseconds and verification in under
    10ms even without optimisation, demonstrating practical deployability.  For
    context, Idemix, a widely deployed anonymous credential system over
    BLS12-381, requires approximately 4ms for signing and 3ms for verification
    without tracing support~\cite{CCS:CamVan02}. Our construction achieves
    comparable verification cost while additionally supporting epoch-bound
    tracing.
